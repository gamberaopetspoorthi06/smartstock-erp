## Aiven MySQL — Environment Variables for Render
## Copy-paste these exact values into Render dashboard → Environment tab

## STEP 1: After creating your Aiven MySQL service, get these values from:
## Aiven Console → Your Service → Overview → Connection Information

SPRING_DATASOURCE_URL=jdbc:mysql://PASTE_AIVEN_HOST:PASTE_PORT/defaultdb?ssl-mode=REQUIRED&serverTimezone=UTC
SPRING_DATASOURCE_USERNAME=avnadmin
SPRING_DATASOURCE_PASSWORD=PASTE_AIVEN_PASSWORD

## STEP 2: These are already set — don't change
SPRING_DATASOURCE_DRIVER=com.mysql.cj.jdbc.Driver
SPRING_JPA_PLATFORM=org.hibernate.dialect.MySQL8Dialect
DB_POOL_SIZE=3
PORT=8080

## STEP 3: After Vercel deploys, paste your live URL here
CORS_ALLOWED_ORIGINS=https://YOUR-PROJECT.vercel.app

## STEP 4: JWT_SECRET — In Render, click "Generate" instead of pasting
## Render will auto-create a cryptographically secure random value
JWT_SECRET=<click_generate_in_render>
